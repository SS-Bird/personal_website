## Overview

The site should be simple and not interfere to much with the user (no scroll hyjacking for example). It should be simple, clean, and have harsh contrast where there is embelishment. It should be minimally decorated but have some celtic braiding decoration, and some embelishment that resembles the unfinished look of oil pastel, crayon, or graffit. The purple should be a minimal embelishment.
