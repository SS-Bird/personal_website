Medieval kirtles

Notes and projects on how they were cut, sewn, laced, and worn.

Projects

A kirtle is the fitted gown worn over a linen chemise. These are my attempts at making them the way they were made.

wool

Blue wool kirtle

A fitted gown in wool twill, with a lined bodice and gores set into the skirt. Shaped by draping on the body instead of using a modern pattern.

View project
lacing

Spiral lacing

Hand-sewn eyelets and one continuous lace, a way of closing a fitted gown that shows up in late medieval art.

View project
dye

Plant-dyed swatches

Wool dyed with weld, madder, and woad: the yellows, reds, and blues medieval dyers could get from plants.
